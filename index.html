Summary

Comparing the two distributions reveals a clear difference in average 5K time improvement between men and women from freshman to senior year. Men improved substantially on average, dropping 1 minute and 12 seconds from 19:34 as freshmen to 18:22 as seniors (with 87% of male runners recording a faster senior time, visible in the clear leftward shift of the red dots). In contrast, women showed a much smaller average improvement of 9 seconds, moving from 22:36 to 22:28 (with 59% running faster as seniors and the freshman and senior distributions largely overlapping).

Data Source: Ohio High School Athletic Association (OHSAA) District Cross Country Championship results (2011–2020), archived on Baum’s Page.
